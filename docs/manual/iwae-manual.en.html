This PDF comes with every new Vault as an example. Highlight it, capture from it and translate it as you read. The “Try it” boxes tell you what to do. Anything you mark while practicing can be undone with Ctrl+Z or deleted at any time.

Vault and screen

IWAE opens a single folder called a Vault.

Your PDFs and Markdown files stay in that folder as they are, and the app keeps its own records in a .iwae folder inside it. A Vault is a plain folder, so you can copy or back it up with your file manager. Nothing is locked inside the app: if you uninstall IWAE, your files are still there.

Inside the Vault folder

PathContents
*.pdf · *.mdYour documents. Subfolders appear in the left list exactly as they are.
Notes/*.iwnoteNote files. A Vault that already has a 노트 folder (the Korean default) keeps using it.
.iwae/App records: highlights, bookmarks, search index, translation cache, history, trash

Opening and organizing documents

  • Click a document in the left “Explorer” to open it in a tab. Create folders, rename and drag items to move them right there.
  • Use “Add document” to import files, or drag documents from your file manager onto the Vault.
  • The GitHub button imports a folder of Markdown documents from a repository (p. 10).
  • A PDF opened straight from your file manager opens without being imported. It stays under “Recent PDFs” at the bottom left, and dragging it onto a Vault folder imports it.
  • Click the Vault name at the bottom left to switch Vaults. Your page and open tabs are remembered next time.

Three parts of the window

Left barDocuments and notes, outline, thumbnails, PDF tools, search. Drag its edge to resize.
CenterTabs for open documents, split into panes to read side by side.
Right note barThe open note. It scrolls separately, and its memos are linked to highlights by lines.
Good to knowOnly two things ever leave your PC: the selected text when you choose a cloud translation service, and requests from an external AI when you turn that connection on yourself. Details are on p. 11.
Try it
  1. In the left “Explorer”, click Markdown example to open it in a new tab.
  2. Click between the tabs to switch between this guide and the Markdown example.
  3. Create a folder with “New folder” and rename it “Practice”. You can delete it afterwards.

Tabs and split view

Every document gets a tab, and panes let you read several side by side.

There are two ways to split the screen, and both use the same nine layouts.

Single
Two side by side
Two stacked
Three side by side
Two top · one bottom
One top · two bottom
One left · two right
Two left · one right
Four (2×2)
Split view (window)The button at the top right. Each pane opens a different document. Press Ctrl+\ to cycle one, two and three panes.
Split view (PDF)The button on the PDF toolbar. See different pages of the same PDF together; each pane has its own page and zoom.

Drag tabs like browser tabs

Drag within a paneReorders the tabs.
Drag to another pane's tab barMoves the tab to that pane.
Drag to a pane edgeDrag a tab to the edge of a pane and a new pane is created on that side.
Drag out of the windowThe PDF tab becomes its own window, showing just the document and the note bar.
Drag onto a tab barClose the detached window or choose “Reattach to the main window” and the tab returns with its page and zoom.

New window (Ctrl+Shift+N) opens a second window on the same Vault. What you highlight or write in one window shows up in the other, and edits from both windows are merged instead of overwriting each other. With two monitors, keep the document on one and the note on the other.

Viewing and moving around

  • Page layout: single page, two pages, three pages or continuous scroll. The bottom bar has fit width, fit page, fit height and actual size (1:1); zoom with Ctrl+wheel.
  • Ctrl+G goes to a page. Hover a link in a document to preview it, click to follow it, and come back with Alt+← or the mouse back button.
Try it
  1. At the top right, open “Split view” and choose “Two side by side”.
  2. Drag the Markdown example tab into the right pane and read the two documents side by side.
  3. Drag a tab out of the window to detach it, then drag it back onto the tab bar to reattach it.

Highlights and memos

Reading and marking happen in one motion. Select text, a highlight is painted, and a memo opens in the note on the right.

Try it 1
  1. On the toolbar, check that “Select text” is on (it is by default).
  2. Drag over a practice sentence below. A highlight appears and a memo opens in the note.
  3. Write a memo, scroll far away, then click the memo card. The PDF returns to the highlighted sentence.

Practice 1When you highlight a sentence you stop at, it moves into a memo card in the note.

Practice 2A highlighted sentence is linked to its memo card by a thin line.

Practice 3Click a memo card and the PDF returns to the highlighted sentence.

When you select text

Drag with Select textPaints a highlight and opens a memo.
Drag with the Hand toolSelects text without highlighting, for copying.
Buttons above a selectionCopy · Copy with source · Search documents · Translate · Send to note
Click a highlightOpens its memo card in the note so you can edit it. With the note bar closed, a small popover lets you change the color, write a memo, send it to the note, or delete it.
Click a memo card's sourceThe PDF jumps to the spot. “Show in source” in the card menu does the same, and “Delete highlight and memo” removes it.

Colors

The “Highlight” tool paints color without a memo. The default colors are the four below. In Settings, under Display & documents, you can add named colors up to eight in total and drag them to reorder. If you delete a color, highlights painted with it stay as “Other”.

Yellow Green Blue Pink Your own colors, up to 8

The connector lines

The note bar is fixed at the right edge of the window, so even with several panes the connector lines cross the other panes to reach their cards. A line has the color of its highlight. The eye button on the toolbar, “Hide all marks”, hides highlights, memos and lines at once. Undo (Ctrl+Z) reverts changes to highlights, drawings and memo notes without touching what another window added.

Try it 2
  1. Switch to the “Highlight” tool, pick a color and paint a few sentences.
  2. Click a highlighted sentence to see its card open in the note, then delete it with “Delete highlight and memo” in the card menu. Hide the marks with the eye button and show them again.

Capture, box memos, drawing

Capture what is not text, such as tables and figures, and write or draw directly on the page.

Region capture

Choose “Region capture” on the toolbar and box a table or figure. It goes into the note as an image with a description field. A captured box can be resized by dragging its edges and corners. Holding Ctrl while dragging also boxes a table or figure. On a scanned page with no text layer, the text recognized by OCR is attached too.

Box memos and drawing

A “Box memo” attaches a memo box in the page margin, joined by a line. “Text box”, “Pen”, the drawing tools (line, arrow, rectangle, ellipse) and “Eraser” write directly on the PDF; a floating format panel sets color and thickness. The eraser removes highlights and box memos as well as drawings.

One-key shortcutsT text box · P pen · L line · A arrow · R rectangle · O ellipse · X redact · E eraser · Esc tool off (outside text fields)
Drawing practice area: draw with the pen, place a text box or attach a box memo here
Try it
  1. Turn on “Region capture” and box Figure 1 above. When the image lands in the note, type a description.
  2. Drag an edge or corner of the box to resize it.
  3. Attach a “Box memo” in the margin, press P and draw in the practice area, then press E to erase. Esc turns the tool off.

Notes and tickets

A note is a document built from blocks. One note can mix memos from several PDFs and Markdown files.

BlockDescription
SectionGroups the blocks under it. Collapse it, or drag it and the blocks under it move together.
TextFree writing in the note, with bold, lists, tables and code.
MemoYour comment with its sources (page numbers) underneath. Click a source to jump to the PDF. A memo can hold several sources, captures included.
TicketA memo with an ID and a title, for tracking requirements.

Drag the handle on the left to change the order of blocks. Select several blocks with Ctrl or Shift+click and drag them together.

ID and title (tickets)

Memos can carry an ID and a title. Hover a memo to see + ID and + Title at the top right, or start the memo with something like REQ-04 to turn it into an ID. IWAE suggests the next number and warns about an ID already used in the note.

Below are practice requirements. Highlight one line at a time to make a memo, then give it an ID.

REQ-01 Saved notes must still be there after the app restarts.
REQ-02 Deleting a highlight must also remove its memo block from the note.
REQ-03 Highlights must point to the same text after pages are reordered.
REQ-04 Text to translate must only be sent where the user chose.

The example note in this Vault already holds REQ-01 and REQ-03 as tickets.

Working with the note bar

Switch noteClick the arrow beside the note title to list this PDF's notes and other notes, and to create a note for this PDF. The magnifier searches all notes.
Notes per PDFExpand the caret on a PDF row in the left “Explorer” to see that PDF's notes under it, and create new ones there.
Show memos onlySpreads the note into a wide column with an outline. Good for organizing and polishing; “Show with document” brings the PDF back.
Expand all memosOpens or closes the quoted source text on every memo card at once. “Insert text block” (+) adds free writing to the note.
Try it
  1. Highlight the REQ-02 sentence to make a memo, then press “+ ID” on it and enter REQ-02. Add a title with “+ Title”.
  2. Turn on “Show memos only” at the top of the note bar, look at the outline, then close it.

Left bar, library, versions

Five tabs at the top of the left bar are how you find and skim documents.

ExplorerThe Vault's documents and notes. Open documents get a dot; the selected document and note are marked.
OutlineThe outline and bookmarks of this PDF. Click one to go to that page.
ThumbnailsPage thumbnails. A rectangle shows the area currently on screen.
PDF toolsOCR, compare, merge and more (p. 9).
SearchSwitch between “This document” and “All documents”. Options for case (Aa) and whole word (W); click a result to open it at that spot.

Searching all documents covers file names, folders, PDF text and Markdown content in one place. Pages without a text layer are read locally with OCR and indexed.

Expand the library

The library button at the top of the left bar (Ctrl+Shift+L) turns the left bar into navigation (All, Favorites, Recently opened, folders) and fills the area on the right with a table of every document.

  • Edit paper details such as title, authors and year in place, sort, star documents and group them into collections. Switch between list and card views.
  • Paper details are filled in from the PDF or Crossref, and you can copy a BibTeX citation.

Versions

Group the draft, revised and final files of one document as its versions and switch between them. Use “Group with another file as versions…” on a file row; ungrouping leaves the files as they are. The PDF tool “Compare text with another version” (p. 9) shows only what changed between two versions, side by side.

Zoom each bar separately

Ctrl+wheel over the left bar, the right note bar or the library zooms each one separately between 80% and 150%. The values are saved and do not affect PDF zoom. The size of the whole interface is set under “Interface size” in Settings.

Try it
  1. In “Outline”, click a heading to jump to a chapter, then watch the rectangle follow in “Thumbnails”.
  2. Press Ctrl+Shift+F, search “All documents” for Vault and click a result.
  3. Expand and collapse the library with Ctrl+Shift+L. Try Ctrl+wheel over the left bar as well.

Translation and Markdown

Translate just the text you select and read it beside the original, which is never replaced.

Translation

Select text and press “Translate” in the buttons that appear; a result card opens with the language, the translation method, retry, and copy with the original. Each source line in a note has a small translate button that shows the translation right under it. Text broken across lines is joined before translating, and text that is already in the target language is translated into another one.

MethodWhat to know
Local (Ollama)Translates on your own PC. Nothing leaves the machine. It can be slow, depending on the model.
CloudOpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Azure Translator or DeepL. The selected text is sent to the service you choose.

Practice 4Cloud API keys are stored in this PC's credential store.

Translation cache: a text translated once into the same language is reused instantly, without another request. Turn it off or clear it under Translation in Settings; translations stay in the Vault's .iwae folder. The method and API keys are set there too.

Markdown documents

Markdown files open in tabs next to your PDFs, with headings, tables, code, task lists and an outline. Highlights, memos and region captures work just as in a PDF, and the memos mix into the same note. Change the look under “Markdown document theme” in Settings; the original file is never changed.

  • From GitHub: the GitHub button above the left “Explorer” imports a folder of Markdown documents from a repository. The top bar shows when there are new commits; pull the latest, or open an older version from a branch, tag or commit.
Try it
  1. Select the Spanish paragraph (Practice 5) and press “Translate”. Translate the same paragraph again and it appears at once thanks to the cache.
  2. Highlight it as a memo, then press the translate button on the card's source line in the note.
  3. On the Markdown example tab, highlight a sentence to add a memo to the same note.

Look, settings, your data

Open Settings with Ctrl+,.

Themes and color

ThemeSystem, Light or Dark. System follows your operating system immediately.
Color schemeDefault, Neutral gray, Warm paper or Deep navy. With “New theme” you set just eight base colors per brightness and the rest are derived, with a live preview and contrast warnings. Themes can be exported and imported as files, duplicated and deleted.
Accent colorBlue, teal, green, purple, rose or amber, or pick any color of your own; you are warned when contrast is too low.
Highlight colorsCreate named colors, up to eight, and drag them to reorder.
Size, languageInterface size 90–125%, Korean, English or system language, and six Markdown document themes.

Your data stays on your PC

Safe savesFiles are written to a temporary file and swapped in atomically, and a journal detects interrupted writes. Earlier states are kept for a limited time, and conflicting edits are kept side by side for you to choose.
TrashDeleted items go to .iwae/trash/ first.
Automatic backupTurn it on under “Data & recovery”. On the days you pick, the whole Vault is zipped into a folder, and only the number of backups you set are kept.
Several windowsWindows on one Vault merge their changes instead of overwriting each other.
What leaves your PCOnly the selected text when you choose a cloud translation service, the update check, and requests from an external AI after you turn that on. The external AI connection is off by default, and when on it listens only on 127.0.0.1 and needs a token.

External AI and updates

  • External AI connection: IWAE has no built-in chat. An outside agent such as Claude Code can use your Vault through an MCP server: search it, read pages, add and change memos. Turn it on under “External AI connection” and run the command shown once. It cannot modify your PDF files.
  • Updates: Under “Updates”, choose automatic checks and pre-downloading. The signature is verified, and installation only starts when you confirm it.
Try it
  1. Open Settings with Ctrl+,, switch to Dark, then click a color scheme and an accent to see the screen change.
  2. Add a named color under highlight colors and use it to highlight a sentence on this page.
  3. Open the command palette with Ctrl+Shift+P and type “dark” or “new window”.
