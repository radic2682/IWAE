Read, highlight, turn it into notes, and jump back to the source.

This guide is a practice field

This PDF comes with every new Vault as an example. Highlight it, capture from it and translate it as you read. The “Try it” boxes tell you what to do. Anything you mark while practicing can be undone with Ctrl+Z or deleted at any time.

  2. Click a memo card. The PDF jumps to the sentence it was written for.
  3. Click a page number in the contents below to go to a chapter, and follow its “Try it” steps.

Vault and screen

IWAE opens a single folder called a Vault.

Your PDFs and Markdown files stay in that folder, and the app keeps its own records in a .iwae folder inside it. A Vault is a plain folder, so you can copy or back it up with your file manager. Nothing is locked inside the app. If you uninstall IWAE, your files are still there.

Inside the Vault folder

PathContents
*.pdf · *.mdYour documents. Subfolders appear in the left list with the same structure.
Notes/*.iwnoteNote files. A Vault that already has a 노트 folder (the Korean default) keeps using it.
.iwae/App records: highlights, bookmarks, search index, translation cache, history, sessions, trash

Opening and organizing documents

  • Click a document in the left “Explorer” to open it in a tab. Create folders, rename and drag items to move them there too.
  • Use “Add document” to import files, or drag documents from your file manager onto the Vault folder.
  • The GitHub button imports a folder of Markdown documents from a repository (p. 10).
  • A PDF opened from your file manager is not imported. It stays under “Recent PDFs” at the bottom left, and dragging it onto a Vault folder imports it.
  • Click the Vault name at the bottom left to switch Vaults. Your page and open tabs are restored next time.

Parts of the window

Top rowThe left bar view tabs, the session name, the screen tabs, the toolbar of the current pane and the translator button.
Left barDocuments and notes, outline, thumbnails, PDF tools, search. Drag its edge to resize.
PanesDocuments and notes are all tabs, and tabs live in panes. Each pane has its own head (color dot, number, tab bar) and tools. A note scrolls in its own pane. Splitting is explained on p. 4.
Good to knowOnly three things leave your PC: text sent to a cloud translation service, the update check, and requests from an external AI after you turn that connection on. Details are on p. 11.
Try it
  1. In the left “Explorer”, click Markdown example to open it in a new tab.
  2. Click the tabs to switch between this guide and the Markdown example.
  3. Create a folder with “New folder” and rename it “Practice”. You can delete it afterwards.

Tabs, panes, screens

Every document gets a tab. Tabs live in panes, and you split panes to read several documents side by side.

PaneWhere tabs gather. Each pane has its own tab bar and tools.
ScreenA set of panes. Switch screens with the screen tabs in the top row (“0 Read”, “1 Compare”).
SessionSeveral screens saved under a name. Switch and save from the session name in the top row.

Split panes with the prefix key

Press the prefix key (Ctrl+B by default), release it, then press one of the keys below. The “⋯” menu in a pane head does the same things. After 2 seconds, or on Esc, it cancels.

Panes% split left-right, " split top-bottom, ← ↑ → ↓ move, z zoom this pane (again to restore), x close, { } swap, Space cycle layouts, q jump by number
Screens! move the pane to a new screen, c new screen, n p next and previous screen, 0 to 9 jump by number
Otherf hide or show floating panes, w open or close the translator, ? keys and commands

If your input method gets in the way, type a command such as split-window -h in the command palette (Ctrl+K).

New screens and floating panes

  • “New screen” in the top row adds a screen. Templates: “Reading”, “Note on the left”, “Compare” and “Blank screen”.
  • “Float” in a pane head's “⋯” menu lifts the pane over the screen. Drag its head to move it and a corner to resize it.

Rearrange by dragging

Drag a tab within a paneReorders the tabs.
Drag a tab to another pane's tab barMoves the tab to that pane.
Drag a tab to a pane edgeDrag a tab to the edge of a pane and a new pane appears on that side.
Drag a tab out of the windowIt becomes a new app window. Drag it back onto a tab bar or choose “Reattach” to return it.
Drag a pane headDrop it on another pane to swap the two, or on an edge to split there and move it.

New window (Ctrl+Shift+N) is a second app window on the same session. What you highlight or write in one shows up in the other, and edits to the same file are merged instead of overwriting each other.

Viewing and moving around

  • Page layout: single page, two pages, three pages or continuous scroll. The bottom bar sets fit and zoom.
  • Ctrl+G goes to a page. Hover a link to preview it and click to follow it.
Try it
  1. Open the “⋯” menu in the document pane head and choose “Split to the right”.
  2. Drag the Markdown example tab into the new pane and read the two documents side by side.
  3. Drag a tab out of the window to detach it, then drag it back onto a tab bar.

Highlights and memos

Reading and marking happen in one motion. Select text, and a memo opens in the note.

Try it 1
  1. On the toolbar, check that “Select text” is on (it is by default). If no note is visible, press Ctrl+Shift+M to open one beside the document.
  2. Drag over a practice sentence below. A highlight appears and a memo opens in the note.
  3. Write a memo, scroll far away, then click the memo card. The PDF returns to the highlighted sentence.

Practice 1When you highlight a sentence you stop at, it becomes a memo card in the note.

Practice 2A highlighted sentence is linked to its memo card by a thin line.

Practice 3Click a memo card and the PDF returns to the highlighted sentence.

When you select text

Drag with Select textPaints a highlight and opens a memo.
Drag with the Hand toolSelects text without highlighting, for copying.
Buttons above a selectionCopy, Copy with source, Search documents, Translate
Click a highlightOpens its memo card in the note so you can edit it, opening the note beside the document if it is not visible. A highlight that is in no note gets a small popover to change the color, write a memo or delete it.
Click a memo card's sourceThe PDF jumps to the spot. “Show in source” in the card menu does the same, and “Delete highlight and memo” removes it.

Colors

The “Highlight” tool paints color without a memo. The default colors are the four below. In Settings, under Display & documents, add named colors (up to eight) and drag them to reorder. If you delete a color, highlights painted with it stay as “Other”.

Yellow Green Blue Pink Your own colors, up to 8

Color channels and connector lines

The color dot in a pane head is its color channel: blue, green, orange or None. Panes with the same color are linked. When a document pane and a note pane share a color, a line in the highlight's color joins each highlight to its memo card, even if the panes are not side by side; where the line crosses another pane it passes over it faintly. Click the dot to change the color, or drag it onto another pane to link it. A card whose document is not shown in any pane of the same color shows “→ document name”. The eye button on the toolbar, “Hide all marks”, hides highlights, memos and lines at once. Undo (Ctrl+Z) reverts changes to highlights, drawings and memo notes without touching what another window added.

Try it 2
  1. Switch to the “Highlight” tool, pick a color and paint a few sentences.
  2. Click a highlighted sentence to see its card open in the note, then delete it with “Delete highlight and memo” in the card menu. Hide the marks with the eye button, then show them again.

Capture, box memos, drawing

Capture what is not text, such as tables and figures, and write or draw on the page.

Region capture

Choose “Region capture” on the toolbar and box a table or figure. It goes into the note as an image with a description field. If no note is open beside the document, the capture is added to the end of the note you used last without opening it, and a notice tells you. A captured box can be resized by dragging its edges and corners. Holding Ctrl while dragging also boxes a table or figure. On a scanned page with no text layer, the text read by OCR is attached too.

Box memos and drawing

A “Box memo” attaches a memo box in the page margin, joined by a line. “Text box”, “Pen”, the drawing tools (line, arrow, rectangle, ellipse) and “Eraser” write on the PDF directly; a format box sets color and thickness. The eraser removes highlights and box memos as well as drawings.

One-key shortcutsT text box · P pen · L line · A arrow · R rectangle · O ellipse · X redact · E eraser · Esc tool off (outside text fields)
Drawing practice area: draw with the pen, place a text box or attach a box memo here
Try it

  2. Drag an edge or corner of the box to resize it.
  3. Attach a “Box memo” in the margin, press P and draw in the practice area, then press E to erase. Esc turns the tool off.

Notes and tickets

A note is a document built from blocks. One note can mix memos from several PDFs and Markdown files.

BlockDescription
SectionGroups the blocks under it. Collapse it, or drag it and the blocks under it move together.
TextFree writing in the note, with bold, lists, tables and code.
MemoYour comment with its sources (page numbers) underneath. Click a source to jump to the PDF. A memo can hold several sources, captures included.
TicketA memo with an ID and a title, for tracking requirements.

Drag the handle on the left to change the order of blocks. Select several blocks with Ctrl or Shift+click and drag them together.

Drop blocks onto a note open in another pane to move them there. Hold Alt when you drop to link instead, and the original note is unchanged. Esc cancels.

ID and title (tickets)

Memos can carry an ID and a title. Hover a memo to see + ID and + Title at the top right, or start the memo with something like REQ-04 to turn it into an ID. IWAE suggests the next number and warns about an ID already used in the note.

Below are practice requirements. Highlight one line at a time to make a memo, then give it an ID.

REQ-01 Saved notes must still be there after the app restarts.
REQ-02 Deleting a highlight must also remove its memo block from the note.
REQ-03 Highlights must point to the same text after pages are reordered.
REQ-04 Text to translate must only be sent where the user chose.

The example note in this Vault already holds REQ-01 and REQ-03 as tickets.

Working with note panes

Open noteClick the arrow beside the note tab title to list this PDF's notes and other notes. The note you pick opens as a tab. You can also create a note for this PDF or move this note to the trash there. The magnifier searches all memos.
Notes per PDFExpand the caret on a PDF row in the left “Explorer” to see that PDF's notes under it, and create new ones there.
Wide viewDrag the note pane wide (about 460px and up) and it becomes a wide column with an outline. Good for organizing and polishing. Narrow it again for the card list.
Expand all memosOpens or closes the quoted source text on every memo card at once. “Insert text block” (+) adds free writing to the note.
Try it
  1. Highlight the REQ-02 sentence to make a memo, then press “+ ID” on it and enter REQ-02. Add a title with “+ Title”.
  2. Drag the note pane wide to see the outline, then narrow it again.

Left bar, library, versions

Five tabs at the left of the top row are how you find and skim documents.

ExplorerThe Vault's documents and notes. Open documents get a dot, and the selected document and note are marked.
OutlineThe outline and bookmarks of this PDF. Click one to go to that page.
ThumbnailsPage thumbnails. A rectangle shows the area now in view.
PDF toolsOCR, compare, merge and more (p. 9).
SearchSwitch between “This document” and “All documents”. Options for case (Aa) and whole word (W). Click a result to open it at that spot.

Searching all documents covers file names, folders, PDF text and Markdown content together. Pages without a text layer are read locally with OCR and indexed.

Expand the library

The library button at the top of the left bar (Ctrl+Shift+L) turns the left bar into navigation (All, Favorites, Recently opened, folders) and fills the area on the right with a table of every document.

  • Edit paper details such as title, authors and year in the table, sort, star documents and group them into collections. Switch between list and card views.
  • Paper details are filled in from the PDF or Crossref, and you can copy a BibTeX citation.

Versions

Group the draft, revised and final files of one document as its versions and switch between them. Use “Group with another file as versions…” on a file row; ungrouping leaves the files as they are. The PDF tool “Compare text with another version” (p. 9) shows only what changed between two versions, side by side.

Zoom each bar separately

Ctrl+wheel over the left bar, a note pane (card list view) or the library zooms each one separately between 80% and 150%. The values are saved and do not affect PDF zoom. The size of the whole interface is set under “Interface size” in Settings.

Try it
  1. In “Outline”, click a heading to go to a chapter, then watch the rectangle follow in “Thumbnails”.
  2. Press Ctrl+Shift+F, search “All documents” for Vault and click a result.
  3. Expand and collapse the library with Ctrl+Shift+L. Try Ctrl+wheel over the left bar as well.

Translation and Markdown

Translate just the text you select and read it beside the original, which is never replaced.

Translation

Select text and press “Translate” in the buttons that appear. A result card opens with the language, the translation method, retry, and copy with the original. Each source line in a note has a small translate button that shows the translation under it. Text broken across lines is joined before translating, and text that is already in the target language is translated into another one.

The translator is a panel that floats over the app window. Open or close it with the translator button in the top row or the prefix key followed by w. It takes text selected in a document or text you type (Ctrl+Enter translates), and you resize it by dragging a corner. Turning off “Use translation” in Settings hides the Translate buttons and the translator.

MethodWhat to know
Local (Ollama)Translates on your own PC. Nothing leaves the machine. It can be slow, depending on the model.
CloudGoogle Translate (no key), Azure Translator, DeepL, OpenAI, Anthropic or Gemini. The selected text is sent to the service you choose.

Practice 4Cloud API keys are stored in this PC's credential store.

Translation cache: a text translated once into the same language is reused from storage, without another request. Turn it off or clear it under Translation in Settings. Translations stay in the Vault's .iwae folder. The method and API keys are set there too.

Markdown documents

Markdown files open in tabs next to your PDFs, with headings, tables, code, task lists and an outline. Highlights, memos and region captures work just as in a PDF, and the memos mix into the same note. Change the look under “Markdown document theme” in Settings; the original file is never changed.

  • From GitHub: the GitHub button above the left “Explorer” imports a folder of Markdown documents from a repository. You are told when there are new commits. Pull the latest, or open an older version from a branch, tag or commit.
Try it
  1. Select the Spanish paragraph (Practice 5) and press “Translate”. Translate the same paragraph again and it comes from the cache.
  2. Highlight it as a memo, then press the translate button on the card's source line in the note.
  3. On the Markdown example tab, highlight a sentence to add a memo to the same note.

Look, settings, your data

Open Settings with Ctrl+,.

Themes and color

ThemeSystem, Light or Dark. System follows your operating system.
Color schemeDefault, Neutral gray, Warm paper or Deep navy. With “New theme” you set just eight base colors per brightness and the rest are derived, with a live preview and contrast warnings. Themes can be exported and imported as files, duplicated and deleted.
Accent colorBlue, teal, green, purple, rose or amber, or pick any color of your own; you are warned when contrast is too low.
Highlight colorsCreate named colors, up to eight, and drag them to reorder.
Size, languageInterface size 90–125%, Korean, English or system language, and six Markdown document themes.
Prefix keyUnder “Shortcuts & mouse”, choose Ctrl+B (default), Ctrl+Space or Ctrl+A, or set your own.

Your data stays on your PC

Safe savesFiles are written to a temporary file and replaced in one step, and a journal detects interrupted writes. Earlier states are kept for a limited time, and conflicting edits are kept side by side for you to choose.
TrashDeleted items go to .iwae/trash/ first.
Automatic backupTurn it on under “Data & recovery”. On the days you pick, the whole Vault is zipped into a folder, and only the number of backups you set are kept.
Several app windowsApp windows on one Vault merge their changes instead of overwriting each other.
API keysKept in the operating system's credential store, not in the Vault.
What leaves your PCOnly text sent to a cloud translation service, the update check, and requests from an external AI after you turn that on. The external AI connection is off by default. When on, it listens only on 127.0.0.1 and needs a token.

External AI and updates

  • External AI connection: IWAE has no built-in chat. An outside agent such as Claude Code can use your Vault through an MCP server: search it, read pages, add and change memos. Turn it on under “External AI connection” and run the command shown once. It cannot change your PDF files.
  • Updates: Under “Updates”, choose automatic checks and pre-downloading. The signature is verified, and installation starts only when you confirm it.
Try it
  1. Open Settings with Ctrl+,, switch to Dark, then click a color scheme and an accent to see the screen change.
  2. Add a named color under highlight colors and use it to highlight a sentence on this page.
  3. Open the command palette with Ctrl+Shift+P and type “dark” or “new window”.
