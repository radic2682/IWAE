# IWAE v0.1.12

A quick fix release for v0.1.11.

## Fixed

- **Highlights showed as solid black** in the installed app. The highlight colors were blocked by the app's content security policy in release builds, so text highlights turned into black bars and region marks into black boxes. Colors are now applied in a way the policy allows; the policy itself is unchanged.
- Memo text colors and highlights inside memo cards, and the page setup used when printing, were affected by the same problem and are fixed too.
- The hover tab of a memo card at the top of the note bar was hidden under the format toolbar. It now draws over the toolbar.

## Improved

- With the note bar open, PDF and Markdown pages are centered with equal left and right margins instead of leaving extra room only on the right.

## Upgrade notes

- Install it from **Settings → Updates** when the app offers it. Your highlights were never changed on disk; they show in their colors again after updating.
