# IWAE v0.2.1

Fixes for the new workspace: tab tear-off, docking at the workspace edge, connector lines and the light theme.

## Changed

- **Connector lines reach every same-color pane.** A memo card and its highlight are now linked whenever their panes share a color, not only when the panes sit side by side. Panes above or below each other are linked too. Where a line passes over another pane it is drawn faintly so the text stays readable.
- **Cleaner connector lines.** Each link is one solid line with a single dot at the highlight, and long lines curve inside the document pane instead of running along pane borders.
- **Memo cards look the same in wide note panes.** Widening a note pane no longer changes the card design. It only centers the cards and shows the outline.
- **Light theme.** Pane heads use the same light gray as the left bar, and the space between panes matches the top bar.

## Fixed

- A tab dragged out of the window now opens a new window only when you release it outside. While it is outside, the tab shows "New window"; moving back in cancels.
- Dropping a tab at the far right, left or bottom edge of the workspace creates a new full-height or full-width column. Existing panes keep their minimum size.
- A note or Markdown tab that was torn off into its own window can be dragged back onto another window's tab bar.
- The text box tool's format bar sits under the pane head instead of covering the page.
- A memo's ticket ID was cut off at the top.
- Stray gray and white lines at the window edges in the light theme.
- The toolbar folded tools into the ⋯ menu even when there was room, and the ⋯ panel was drawn broken.
