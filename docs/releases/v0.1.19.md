# IWAE v0.1.19

An AI pointer for locations the AI shows, a filter for highlights of other notes, and a better translator.

## New

- **AI pointer.** When an AI connected over MCP points at a sentence (`show_location`, or `goto` with flash), the PDF now marks it with a dashed violet outline and a small AI tag instead of a normal selection. It is temporary and is never saved. Click the page or press Esc to clear it. Saved highlights, including ones the AI created, look the same as always.
- **Show only the open note's highlights.** A new funnel button next to "Hide all marks" in the PDF toolbar hides highlights and memos that belong to other notes, so only the note you are working on shows on the page. It only changes what is drawn; nothing is modified.
- **Floating translator.** A new translator button in the PDF and Markdown toolbars opens a translator that stays open inside the document pane. Type or paste text, press Ctrl+Enter, and copy the result. "Insert selection" pastes what you selected in the document. You can move and resize it.

## Changed

- **Translation card.** You can drag the card that opens after translating a selection by its header.
- **Language bar.** The card and the floating translator now have a source language, a target language and a swap button. Swapping in the translator moves the result into the input and translates it back.
- **The AI activity pill also shows while an AI is only reading.** It now appears for searches, page reads and document lists as "AI is looking", not just for edits.
