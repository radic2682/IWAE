# IWAE v0.1.17

A faster note bar when a note has hundreds of memos.

## Changed

- **Large notes stay smooth.** Memo cards now re-render only when they change, and cards outside the visible area skip layout and painting. With 1,000 memos (measured in a development build), scrolling the PDF went from about 550 ms to 20 ms per frame, typing in a memo from 117 ms to 29 ms per keystroke, hovering cards from about 1 s to 75 ms, and creating a highlight no longer freezes the app for seconds. Opening a PDF with such a note and switching notes are several times faster.
