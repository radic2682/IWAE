# IWAE v0.1.15

Two fixes from user feedback.

## Fixed

- **Clicking a memo on a scanned PDF now opens it.** On pages without a text layer, clicking a highlight or region box did nothing; you had to drag to reach the memo. A single click now opens the memo, and switches to its note when it belongs to another note, just like on a page with text.
- **Ctrl+Z after picking a custom colour.** Changing a text box colour with the colour picker left focus in the picker, so Ctrl+Z was ignored. Undo now works from colour pickers, checkboxes and sliders; text fields keep their own text undo.
